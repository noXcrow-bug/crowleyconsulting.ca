What Windsor's demolition checklist asks

The City of Windsor's demolition permit checklist (Form A.8.1) asks seven environmental questions: past industrial use of the site, whether there is a list of designated substances for the site and building, fluid storage tanks, outstanding Ministry of the Environment or Ministry of Labour orders, PCBs, ozone-depleting substances, and WHMIS hazardous products.

Answer yes to any of them and the checklist says an environmental consultant must complete an Environmental Building Audit, with a dust control plan, before you apply. In a building from before 1990, the designated substances question is almost always a yes.

Our pre-demolition survey answers the designated substance, PCB and refrigerant questions in one report you can attach to both the permit application and the demolition tender.

Sources: City of Windsor demolition permit checklist (Form A.8.1). Check the current form with the building department before you apply.

Windsor's buildings

About two in five Windsor homes were built before 1961, and roughly three in four before 1991, by the 2021 Census. The oldest stock sits in and around the core: Walkerville, Sandwich and the neighbourhoods near downtown.

The city's auto-era plants, warehouses, schools and churches are where the checklist's industrial-use question matters. Pipe and boiler insulation, PCB light ballasts and old refrigeration equipment all need to be found and listed before anyone cuts.

More than a third of Windsor households rent, so suite turnovers never stop. A suite check before a unit is renovated tells the contractor what's in the walls, ceilings and floors.

Questions people ask

Do I need a designated substance survey for a demolition permit in Windsor?

In almost every older building, yes. The City of Windsor's demolition checklist asks whether there is a list of designated substances for the site and building, and any yes on its environmental questions means an Environmental Building Audit before you apply. We write the survey ready to attach to the permit application and the tender.

What is the Environmental Building Audit on Windsor's checklist?

It's the consultant's report the City asks for when any of the checklist's environmental questions is answered yes: industrial use, designated substances, tanks, outstanding orders, PCBs, ozone-depleting substances or hazardous products. It has to include a dust control plan and be finished before the demolition permit application goes in.

How much does asbestos testing cost in Windsor?

A limited sampling visit for one to three materials starts at $650, and a project-specific designated substance report starts at $1,500. Each report is priced as a report fee plus the samples taken, with the first layer of every sample analyzed by the lab included, so the total depends on the building.

How long do results take?

Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site.

Are you independent of abatement contractors?

Yes. We never remove or abate anything and take no referral fees, so our survey results aren't shaped by anyone who profits from removal.
