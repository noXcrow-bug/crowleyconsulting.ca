A register summarises the surveys and condition checks listed for each building and is kept under O. Reg. 278/05 and section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act. It doesn't replace the full survey reports. Materials hidden in walls, ceilings or equipment may not have been seen: if a suspect material turns up during work, stop and call.
