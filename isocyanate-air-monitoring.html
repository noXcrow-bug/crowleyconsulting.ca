Isocyanate air monitoring

Collision and auto body paint booths, paint lines, spray foam installers and polyurethane manufacturers work with isocyanates, one of Ontario's designated substances.

Take the Obligation CheckBook a call

What the regulation asks

  • A written exposure assessment made with your JHSC, redone after any change that could significantly affect exposure
  • A control program where health may be affected
  • Air monitoring, with results posted for 14 days, copied to the JHSC and kept 5 years
  • Medical surveillance, which applies to isocyanates

What we offer

We're not taking on ongoing workplace programs right now. A day of air monitoring during normal spraying, with a short written report, is available as a one-off service, quoted in writing per job.
