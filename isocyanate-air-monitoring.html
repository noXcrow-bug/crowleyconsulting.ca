Isocyanate air monitoring

Collision and auto body paint booths, auto-sector paint lines, spray foam installers and polyurethane manufacturers work with isocyanates, one of Ontario's designated substances. We assess the exposure and monitor the air.

What the regulation asks

  • A written exposure assessment made with your JHSC, redone after any change that could significantly affect exposure
  • A control program where health may be affected
  • Air monitoring by or under a qualified hygienist, results posted for 14 days, copied to the JHSC and kept 5 years
  • Medical surveillance, which applies to isocyanates

What a monitoring day includes

A full day of personal and area air monitoring during normal spraying, a results report, a posting notice and a copy for your JHSC. $1,400 a day, lab analysis included.

The Workplace Exposure Program Shop tier covers the annual assessment review, one monitoring day, a training session and your records for $299 a month, about what the same year costs bought one service at a time.
