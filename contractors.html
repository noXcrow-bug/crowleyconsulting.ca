Testing and clearance for contractors

Test the materials before your crew cuts, get someone on site the same or next business day when work uncovers something, and book clearance air testing when a Type 3 enclosure is ready to come down.

Check what applies to youBook a call

The problem it solves

On small jobs the owner rarely has a current list. The law puts that duty on the owner, but you're the one standing in the room when a suspect material turns up.

When unlisted material is found, work has to stop except to identify it, and the Ministry, the owner, the contractor and the JHSC must be notified right away. Standard lab results take 10 business days, so a find on day one idles your crew or pushes the job into asbestos procedures by presumption.

Testing before you price the job moves that find to the quote, where the owner pays for it.

What we do for contractors

  • Bulk Material Sampling: we test the few materials your work will disturb, with chain of custody and accredited lab analysis.
  • A short letter report for each job: the result per material, photos, and the work classification if a result is positive.
  • An unexpected-find call-out the same or next business day when work uncovers suspect material, with rush lab results in 2–3 business days.
  • Written interim instructions on site if the job can't wait for results: leave the suspect materials alone, or proceed treating them as asbestos.
  • Clearance air testing for Type 3 enclosures, sold directly to abatement contractors.
  • Type 1 and Type 2 instruction and training for your crew, at your site.

Each report is addressed to the building owner, because it's the owner's list. You're named as requester and get a copy.

Clearance for abatement contractors

Type 3 work needs a clearance air test before the enclosure comes down: a final visual inspection, then air samples at the Table 3 count for the enclosure size, analysed on rush. We sell it directly to abatement contractors.

Our fee doesn't depend on the result, and the result is reported as found. We never remove or abate anything and take no referral fees.
