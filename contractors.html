Cleared-to-Start Pass

The contractor compliance desk. One number to call before you bid or cut: we review the owner's designated substance report for gaps, and when a job needs testing we're on site faster and you pay the member rate.

Check what applies to youBook a call

The problem it solves

On small jobs the owner rarely has a current list. The law puts that duty on the owner, but you're the one standing in the room when a suspect material turns up.

When unlisted material is found, work has to stop except to identify it, and the Ministry, the owner, the contractor and the JHSC must be notified right away. Lab results take 5 business days, so a find on day one idles your crew or pushes the job into asbestos procedures by presumption.

Checking before you price the job moves that find to the quote, where the owner pays for it.

What you get

  • A compliance desk to call or email during business hours.
  • Pre-bid reviews: send us the owner's designated substance report before you price the job, and we tell you whether it covers your work.
  • When it doesn't, a pre-work check at your member rate: a limited sampling visit or a project report for the work area. Put the independent list price on your quote and the owner pays it.
  • Written interim instructions on site if the client can't wait for results: leave the suspect materials alone, or proceed treating them as asbestos. You choose, and the owner gets a copy.
  • 25% off work-classification letters, and a due-diligence file for every job.

“Cleared to start” means the work area was checked before work began. It is not Type 3 clearance air testing, which the building owner retains separately, and we never do clearance on work a Pass holder abates.
