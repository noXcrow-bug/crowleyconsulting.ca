Surveying a building that's open

  • We work around tenants, shifts and production, and book mechanical rooms, roofs and ceiling spaces with your maintenance staff.
  • Samples are small. Each spot is wetted, sealed and patched, and labelled where it was taken.
  • After-hours or weekend fieldwork at your request is +50% on the fieldwork portion of the fee.
  • Areas we can't get into are named in the report. Materials there that could contain asbestos are treated as asbestos-containing until they're inspected, so nobody assumes they match the rooms we did see.

What we need to quote

  • The address, the year it was built and any additions

  • The work planned and where, or the reason for the survey (purchase, tender, demolition)

Questions people ask

Do commercial buildings need an asbestos survey in Ontario?

Two rules apply. Before any renovation, repair or demolition project, section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act requires the owner to list the designated substances in the work area. And where a building has asbestos-containing material, O. Reg. 278/05 requires the owner to keep a record of it, re-inspect it at least every 12 months and have an asbestos management program.

Can you survey while the building is open?

Yes. We schedule around tenants and production, keep sampling small and sealed, and can work after hours or on weekends at your request.

Do my tenants have to be told?

Where there is asbestos-containing material, the owner has to give written notice to occupiers about the asbestos in the areas they occupy, and tell contractors before work that could disturb it. Our reports and registers give you what to send.

Do you survey industrial plants?

Yes: process areas, boiler houses, warehouses and offices. Tell us about current and past processes, because some designated substances, like isocyanates or benzene, come from what a plant did rather than what it's built of.

How much does a commercial asbestos survey cost?

A renovation-scope survey starts at $1,000 and a full-building survey at $1,500, by building size, plus samples at $100 each. We confirm the fee and the estimated samples in writing before any work starts.
