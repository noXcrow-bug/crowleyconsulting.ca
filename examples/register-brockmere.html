Before anyone drills, cuts or lifts a tile

Check the material in this register. If it says Act now or Monitor, or the material isn't listed, call before work starts. A written desk review comes back within 1 business day.

Photos show each material where it was surveyed or sampled, to help you recognise it. A material that looks the same elsewhere is not confirmed by the photo alone: if it isn't listed for that location, call before disturbing it. This register summarises the surveys and condition checks listed for each building and is kept under O. Reg. 278/05 and section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act. It does not replace the full survey reports. Materials hidden in walls, ceilings or equipment may not have been seen: if a suspect material turns up during work, stop and call. Quantities in metres with imperial in brackets. Lab analysis by an accredited laboratory (PLM, 0.5% asbestos threshold).
