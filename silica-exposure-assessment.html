Silica exposure assessment

Countertop fabricators, foundries, concrete products and glass plants carry O. Reg. 490/09 duties for silica. We write the assessment with your JHSC, set up the control program and run the air monitoring.

Take the Obligation CheckBook a call

Why silica is in focus

Engineered stone can be up to 95% silica, and Ontario's Ministry of Labour issued a safety alert on silica exposure during countertop manufacturing and installing in 2023, updated in May 2025.

What you need on file

  • A written exposure assessment made with your joint health and safety committee
  • A control program where health may be affected: wet cutting and ventilation, work and hygiene practices, training
  • Air monitoring by or under a qualified hygienist, results posted for 14 days and kept 5 years
  • Medical surveillance arranged with your physician
  • A respiratory protection program if respirators are in use

Price

Assessments run $2,500 to $7,500 depending on processes. A control program is $2,000 to $5,000 and an exposure monitoring day is $1,400. The Workplace Exposure Program keeps it all current from $299 a month.

Who does the monitoring

Monitoring is done by or under our qualified occupational hygienist. Medical surveillance is done by your physician; we coordinate the records.
