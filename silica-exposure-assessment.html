Silica on renovation and demolition projects

Cutting, grinding or coring concrete, masonry and tile releases respirable crystalline silica, one of Ontario's 11 designated substances. If the work will disturb it, it belongs on the project's designated substance list.

Where it shows up

Concrete, mortar, brick, block, ceramic tile, drywall and plaster all contain silica. Every project-specific report we write records where silica-containing materials are and what the planned work will do to them.

What the contractor needs

  • The designated substance list, with silica-generating work identified
  • Work classified by type, with controls such as wet cutting and local exhaust
  • Training and respiratory protection where the work calls for it

We write work-classification letters for silica and lead as a project service.
