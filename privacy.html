Privacy notice

Noah Crowley Consulting, operating as Noah Crowley Asbestos and Designated Substances Consulting, collects only the personal information you choose to send us through this website.

What we collect and why

When you send a form or the Obligation Check result, we receive your name, company, email, phone, site address and the answers or details you provide. We use them to reply, to quote and to deliver the service you ask for.

Email

We send commercial emails only with your consent or where Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation otherwise allows it. The reminders checkbox on our forms is optional and unticked by default. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and we act on unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to us by our form provider, and calls are booked through our scheduling provider. Fonts are served from this website, not a third party. We don't sell or rent personal information, and this site does not use advertising trackers.

Keeping and correcting it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal and insurance obligations. You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us at info@crowleyconsulting.ca.
