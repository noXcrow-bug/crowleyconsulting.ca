Questions people ask

How much does asbestos testing cost in London?

A project designated substance survey is $550 for one suite or up to 2,000 sq ft, and $1,300 to $4,800 for commercial work areas. Lab analysis is included, so there's no separate testing charge.

How long do results take?

Lab results come back within 1 week of the site visit. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site the same day.

Which buildings in London are likely to contain asbestos?

Most buildings built after 1980 are largely free of asbestos; nearly anything older is a candidate. Floor tile and mastic, drywall compound, textured ceilings, pipe insulation and vermiculite are the usual suspects.

Are you independent of abatement contractors?

Yes. We never remove or abate anything and take no referral fees, so our survey results aren't shaped by anyone who profits from removal.
