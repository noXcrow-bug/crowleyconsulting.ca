What London's demolition form leaves out

The City of London's demolition clearances form covers heritage review, utility sign-offs and a security deposit: $2,000 for a house, or $10 per square metre of ground floor for other buildings, up to $10,000. It doesn't ask about designated substances.

That doesn't remove the duty. Under Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act, the owner must find out which designated substances are present before a renovation or demolition is tendered, and give that list to every contractor who bids. A contractor who bids without that list carries the risk when the walls come open.

Heritage adds time. For a property on London's Register of Cultural Heritage Resources, the owner must give council at least 60 days' written notice before demolishing, and council can move to designate it instead. Have the survey done during that window so it's ready when the permit is.

Sources: City of London required clearances for demolition · City of London demolition control policy. Check the current form with the building department before you apply.

Questions people ask

London's demolition form doesn't mention designated substances. Do I still need a survey?

Yes. The duty comes from the Occupational Health and Safety Act, not the permit form. Under Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act, the owner must find out which designated substances are present before a renovation or demolition is tendered, and give that list to every contractor who bids.

How long does demolishing a heritage-listed property in London take?

At least 60 days from the owner's written notice to council, and longer if council decides to designate the property. London's heritage planner reviews it as part of the demolition clearances.

How much does asbestos testing cost in London?

A limited sampling visit for one to three materials starts at $650, and a project-specific designated substance report starts at $1,500. Each report is priced as a report fee plus the samples taken, with the first layer of every sample analyzed by the lab included, so the total depends on the building.

How long do results take?

Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site.
