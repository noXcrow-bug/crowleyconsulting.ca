Samples and layers

A sample is what we take from a material. The lab analyzes every layer in it: a floor tile and its glue are two layers, a plaster wall with a skim coat can be three. Your quote includes an allowance of layers sized to the building. If a building needs more, extra layers are billed per layer, and we tell you on site before we take them.

Terms

  • Deposit at booking: 50% on projects of $5,000 or more before HST, 30% under $5,000. The balance is due 30 days after the invoice.
  • Minimum charge for a site visit: $450 before HST.
  • Travel is free within 60 km of Windsor.
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days.

After the report

If materials have to come out, we write the abatement specification contractors bid on, review the bids, inspect the work and test the air, including Type 3 clearance. These are quoted per project. We never do the removal ourselves.

Lab turnaround

Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples. We don't offer rush analysis. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site so it can continue lawfully.
