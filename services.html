Reports and prices

Every survey covers Ontario's 11 designated substances and is priced as a report fee by building size, plus the samples taken. Samples are $100 each ($150 on a demolition survey), and extra layers are $45 each. Rush sample prices are double the standard sample price. These are starting prices: larger or more complex jobs are quoted above them.

Samples and layers

A sample is what we take from a material. The lab analyzes every layer in it: a floor tile and its glue are two layers, a plaster wall with a skim coat can be three. Each sample is $100 ($150 on a demolition survey) and includes lab analysis of its first layer. Extra layers are $45 each, billed per layer analyzed. Vermiculite samples are $125 each.

Rush sample prices are double the standard sample price, extra layers included: a $100 sample is $200 on rush, and a $45 extra layer is $90. Rush results take 2–3 business days.

Training and second opinions

Questions people ask

How much does a designated substance survey cost?

Surveys start at $1,000 for one work area, $1,500 for a whole building and $1,750 for a pre-demolition survey, by building size. Samples are added on top: $100 each ($150 on a demolition survey), including lab analysis of the first layer, and $45 for each extra layer. All prices are CAD before HST.

Why is it priced per sample?

The lab analyzes every layer in a sample, and buildings differ: a floor tile and its glue are two layers, a plastered wall can be three. Pricing per sample means you pay for what your building actually needs, and we ask before going over the top of the estimate on your quote.

How long does a survey take?

A renovation-scope survey takes 15 business days, a full-building survey 15–20 and a pre-demolition survey 20–25, from the end of fieldwork. Lab results take 10 business days from when the lab receives the samples; rush takes 2–3 business days at double the sample price.

Is there a minimum charge or travel fee?

The minimum charge for a site visit is $450 before HST. Travel is free within 60 km [37 mi] of Riverside Dr. and Ouellette Ave. (downtown Windsor); beyond that it's $0.75 a km for each km past 60, counted both ways, by road.

Do you do the asbestos removal?

No. We write the abatement specification, review bids, inspect the work and test the air, including Type 3 clearance, but we never remove anything and take no referral fees.
