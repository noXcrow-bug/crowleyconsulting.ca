Reports and prices

Every report covers Ontario's 11 designated substances and is priced as a report fee plus the samples taken. Your quote sets the report fee and an estimated range of samples for your building, and you pay for the samples actually taken. Samples are $100 each ($150 on a demolition survey), and extra layers are $45 each. These are the starting prices. Program members pay 10% to 20% less on the report fee and our time on each sample; lab costs and extra layers are not discounted.

Samples and layers

A sample is what we take from a material. The lab analyzes every layer in it: a floor tile and its glue are two layers, a plaster wall with a skim coat can be three. Each sample is $100 ($150 on a demolition survey) and includes lab analysis of its first layer. Extra layers are $45 each, billed per layer analyzed. Demolition surveys take more samples, because walls, floors and ceilings are opened up.

After the report

If materials have to come out, we write the abatement specification contractors bid on, review the bids, inspect the work and test the air, including Type 3 clearance. These are quoted per project. We never do the removal ourselves.

Lab turnaround

Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples. We don't offer rush analysis. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site so it can continue lawfully.

Questions people ask

How much does a designated substance report cost?

Reports start at $650 for a limited sampling visit and $1,500 for a project-specific designated substance report. Each report is a report fee plus the samples taken: $100 a sample ($150 on a demolition survey), including lab analysis of the first layer, and $45 for each extra layer. All prices are CAD before HST.

Why is it priced per sample?

The lab analyzes every layer in a sample, and buildings differ: a floor tile and its glue are two layers, a plastered wall can be three. Pricing per sample means you pay for what your building actually needs, and we ask before going over the top of your quoted range.

How long does a report take?

From about 5 business days for a DSS update without sampling to 15–25 business days for a pre-demolition survey. Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples, and we don't offer rush analysis.

Is there a minimum charge or travel fee?

The minimum charge for a site visit is $450 before HST. Travel is free within 60 km [37 mi] of Windsor.

Do you do the asbestos removal?

No. We write the abatement specification, review bids, inspect the work and test the air, including Type 3 clearance, but we never remove anything and take no referral fees.
