Designated substance surveys in Windsor and southwestern Ontario

A designated substance survey (DSS) finds and lists the designated substances in a building or work area before a project starts, so the owner meets section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act and every bidder prices the same job. We survey offices, plazas, plants, schools, churches, clinics and apartment buildings across Windsor-Essex, Chatham-Kent, Sarnia-Lambton and London.

When you need one

  • Before a renovation, repair or demolition project is tendered or a contract is signed. The owner has to list every designated substance in the work area first.
  • For a demolition permit. Windsor's demolition checklist asks for the list; see surveys for a demolition permit.
  • When you buy a building or plan capital work, so the cost of what's in it is known before you commit.
  • When there's no survey on file, or the one you have doesn't cover the area the work will touch. A full-building survey also becomes the base for your asbestos record.

Building age changes how big the job is, never whether a list is needed. A newer building usually has fewer suspect materials and a smaller report; it still needs one.

What the report contains

One report, one data source, so the summary, the register and the plans never contradict each other.

  • At a glance and a summary of findings written so it can go straight into tender documents: friable, non-friable and presumed asbestos-containing materials, materials awaiting lab results, lead, and any other designated substance, each with its sample numbers and quantity
  • Building and scope: the areas surveyed, the areas not accessed (materials there that could contain asbestos are treated as asbestos-containing until inspected), and the earlier reports and drawings we reviewed
  • How the survey was done: homogeneous materials sampled to at least the minimum in Table 1 of O. Reg. 278/05, chain of custody and analysis by a CALA-accredited lab
  • Plans: where each sample was taken and where the asbestos-containing materials are
  • A register of every material: location, condition, quantity in metric with imperial beside it, and what to do with it
  • All 11 designated substances addressed, with lead in paint reported against the EACO Lead Guideline classes
  • Before work starts: how to handle what was found, and the owner's duties under Ontario law
  • Lab certificates and a photo log, limitations and sign-off

Every report comes as a PDF with one printed binder. See an example register built from the same kind of data.

Questions people ask

What is a designated substance survey?

A survey that identifies which of Ontario's 11 designated substances are in a building or work area, where they are and how much there is, so the owner can give the list to contractors before a project. Asbestos, lead, mercury and silica are the ones usually found in buildings.

Is a designated substance survey required in Ontario?

Before a project, yes. Section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act requires the project owner to find out which designated substances are present and give the list to the constructor and every bidder before a binding contract. There's no building-age or size cutoff.

What's the difference between a DSS and an asbestos survey?

An asbestos survey looks only for asbestos. A designated substance survey covers all 11 designated substances, which is what section 30 asks the owner to list. Every survey we write is a designated substance survey.

How long is a designated substance survey good for?

The law doesn't set an expiry date. What matters is whether the report covers the area and the work now planned, and whether it still matches the building. If the scope has grown or materials have changed since, the report needs to be extended or redone.

Who pays for the survey?

The project owner, because the duty is the owner's. A contractor can arrange it on the owner's behalf, and the report is addressed to the owner.

Do you also remove the asbestos?

No. We survey, test, write abatement specifications, oversee the work and test the air, but we never remove anything and take no referral fees.
