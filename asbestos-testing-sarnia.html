What Sarnia's demolition application asks

The City of Sarnia's demolition application checklist asks applicants to check for designated substances under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, and points owners to the Ministry of Labour's rules on what must happen before demolition, alteration or repair is tendered.

It also flags past uses that can leave other hazards behind, naming dentist offices and veterinary clinics, and sets a separate heritage step: a property on the municipal heritage register needs 60 days' written notice to council before it's demolished.

Our survey covers all 11 designated substances. We record the building's past uses in the report and flag anything outside those 11 for the right specialist.

Sources: City of Sarnia demolition application (Feb 2026) · Imperial 2023 turnaround (Sarnia News Today). Check the current form with the building department before you apply.

Questions people ask

Does Sarnia need a designated substance report before demolition?

Sarnia's demolition application checklist asks you to check for designated substances under the Occupational Health and Safety Act. Under Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act, the owner must find out which designated substances are present before a renovation or demolition is tendered, and give that list to every contractor who bids. We write the report ready for both the application and the tender.

My building used to be a dental office or a vet clinic. Does that matter?

Sarnia's demolition checklist names those past uses as signs that radioactive materials may have been used on site. That's outside the 11 designated substances, so tell us about past uses when you book. We note them in the report and tell you who handles the follow-up.

How much does asbestos testing cost in Sarnia?

A limited sampling visit for one to three materials starts at $650, and a project-specific designated substance report starts at $1,500. Each report is priced as a report fee plus the samples taken, with the first layer of every sample analyzed by the lab included, so the total depends on the building.

How long do results take?

Lab results take 5 business days from when the lab receives the samples. If work can't wait, we leave written interim instructions on site.
