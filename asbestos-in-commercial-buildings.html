Where asbestos hides in commercial and industrial buildings

Canada banned most asbestos products on December 30, 2018, so any building material put in before then can contain it. In commercial and industrial buildings these are the materials that turn up most, and the work that disturbs them.

"Friable" means it can be crumbled or powdered by hand pressure when dry. Friability is decided on site for each material, and it changes how the work is classified under O. Reg. 278/05.

What clears a material, and what doesn't

A material is only left off the sampling list when it can't contain asbestos because of what it's made of (bare metal, glass, solid wood, poured concrete, fibreglass batt), or when it's a documented product: we know what it is, where and when it went in, and that it was made without asbestos or installed on or after December 30, 2018. Looking new, or being in a newer building, never clears a material on its own.

Windsor's buildings

Windsor-Essex has a large stock of auto-era plants, warehouses, schools, churches and plazas built before the 1990s. Boiler houses and process areas tend to hold the friable materials; offices and schools hold the tile, ceiling and plaster finds. A commercial survey finds them before your contractor does.

Questions people ask

How do I know if a material contains asbestos?

Only a lab result tells you, or you treat the material as asbestos-containing. Suspect materials are sampled and analyzed by a CALA-accredited lab; anything not sampled is presumed to contain asbestos.

Is asbestos in a commercial building illegal?

No. Asbestos-containing material that's in good condition can stay in place. The owner has to record it, re-inspect it at least every 12 months, notify occupiers and contractors, and manage it under O. Reg. 278/05.

Our building was built in the 1990s. Can it still contain asbestos?

It can. Asbestos products stayed on the Canadian market until the end of 2018, and older materials were reused or left in place during renovations. Age changes how much we expect to find, not whether to look.

What does testing cost?

Testing a few materials starts at $450 for the visit plus $100 a sample. A survey of a work area starts at $1,000 plus samples.
