Ontario's 11 designated substances, and where they turn up in buildings

Ontario regulates 11 designated substances. Four of them are found in ordinary buildings; the rest come mostly from industrial processes. A designated substance survey has to consider all 11, so every survey we write does.

Often in the same report, though not designated

PCBs in older fluorescent light ballasts, transformers and some caulking, ozone-depleting refrigerants in old cooling equipment, and mould. They aren't designated substances, but demolition and renovation paperwork often asks about them. We add them to a survey for $350, with any samples at the usual sample prices.

Which rule applies

  • Before a project: section 30 of the Occupational Health and Safety Act. The owner lists the designated substances in the work area before tendering or contracting.
  • Asbestos in buildings: O. Reg. 278/05, which sets the owner's record, re-inspection and notice duties and how asbestos work is classified. See asbestos management programs.
  • Workplaces that use a designated substance: O. Reg. 490/09, which covers exposure assessments and control programs. It doesn't apply on construction projects.

Questions people ask

What are Ontario's 11 designated substances?

Acrylonitrile, arsenic, asbestos, benzene, coke oven emissions, ethylene oxide, isocyanates, lead, mercury, silica and vinyl chloride.

Which designated substances are found in buildings?

Asbestos, lead, mercury and silica, in almost every older building. Isocyanates and arsenic show up in some. The others are tied to industrial processes.

Does a designated substance survey test for all 11?

It considers all 11. Materials that can contain asbestos or lead are sampled for the lab; mercury devices are recorded where they're found; silica-containing materials are noted where the work will disturb them; and the process substances are covered through the building's current and past uses.

Are PCBs a designated substance?

No, but they're often asked about on demolition and renovation projects. We can add PCBs, ozone-depleting refrigerants and mould to a survey for $350.
