How we work

Noah Crowley Asbestos and Designated Substances Consulting is the online name of Noah Crowley Consulting, an independent consultancy covering all 11 of Ontario's designated substances across Windsor-Essex, Chatham-Kent, Sarnia-Lambton and London.

Independence

We never remove or abate anything and take no referral fees. When an abatement contractor hires us for a clearance air test, our fee doesn't depend on the result, and the result is reported as found. It's written into our terms.

One named consultant

You deal with the same person who walks your building, writes your report and answers your pre-work questions.

How pricing works

Surveys are priced as a report fee by building size plus the samples taken. Your quote gives an estimated number of samples, you pay for the samples actually taken, and we ask on site before going over the estimate. Samples are $100 each ($150 on a demolition survey), including lab analysis of the first layer; extra layers are $45 each. Lab results take 10 business days; rush takes 2–3 at double the sample price.

The Annual Asbestos Reassessment is a yearly fee per building, paid a year in advance. It keeps your record current; it never includes a designated substance survey.
